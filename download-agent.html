  • Open apps (Chrome, Word, Notepad, etc.)

Safety

  • Runs only actions on an approved allowlist
  • Tied to your Nova account — no one else can use it
  • You can pause it anytime with the kill switch
  • Login is stored locally at %APPDATA%\Nova\config.json

Frequently asked

What does Nova Agent actually do?
It's a small helper app that runs on your laptop. When you say something to Nova on the website, the agent does the actual work — opening apps, changing your volume, taking screenshots, etc.
Do I have to open it every time?
No. After you sign in the first time, the agent adds itself to your laptop's startup. Every time you turn on your laptop, it starts silently in the background. You never open it again.
Do I have to keep the browser open?
Yes — the website is where you talk to Nova. But the agent itself runs in the background, so you don't need to keep any terminal or window open.
How do I know it's running?
Open Task Manager (Ctrl + Shift + Esc) and look for NovaAgent.exe. Or check this page — if the green banner above says "running", it's alive.
Windows blocked it — what now?
Click "More info" → "Run anyway". This appears because the .exe isn't code-signed (it's a student project, not a commercial app). It's safe.
Is it safe?
Yes. The agent only runs a fixed list of actions — opening apps, adjusting volume, taking screenshots. It cannot read files, run shell commands, or do anything else. It's tied to your Nova account only.
How do I stop or uninstall it?
Stop it: Task Manager → find NovaAgent.exe → End task. Uninstall completely: create an empty file named nova.remove_startup on your Desktop, then reboot — this removes auto-start. You can also delete the file from wherever you saved it.
Where is my login stored?
Locally on your laptop at %APPDATA%\Nova\config.json. It contains your Nova user ID and a login token. Nothing else. Never sent anywhere except Nova's backend.
